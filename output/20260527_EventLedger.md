## Tier Evaluation

### Colorado SB 26-189 (signed May 14, effective Jan 1, 2027)

This is now confirmed in detailed legal analyses (ArentFox Schiff, Akin/Dickinson Wright, others). Standing view already incorporates Colorado's deregulatory move, but today's feeds add operational specificity: effective date Jan 1, 2027; narrowed scope from broad "high-risk AI" to "automated decision-making technology used in consequential decisions"; transparency-and-disclosure framework only (no mandatory bias audits); advance notice obligations. This is the actionable detail layer beneath last week's policy summary.

**Materiality:** 3-4. The substitution is the most consequential US state-level deregulatory event in the current cycle and materially changes vendor/deployer compliance scope in Colorado. Already partially priced into standing view. **Tier 2** — worth ledger entry, no brief inclusion.

### California EO N-6-26 (signed May 21)

Newsom directed state agencies to study AI workforce disruption and propose worker protection policies. This is a study/policy-development order, not an operational mandate. Forward-looking signal but no immediate compliance obligation.

**Materiality:** 2-3. **Tier 2** — flag for watchlist, no ledger entry warranted as standalone.

### HHS AERO Initiative (launched May 21)

HHS is using AI to retrospectively re-score 5+ years of Single Audit Act compliance data for federally-funded entities receiving >$1M annually. Hospitals receiving Medicaid, NIH, HRSA, or pandemic-relief funding are directly affected. This is a **government deployment of AI as an enforcement/audit tool**, which is structurally significant: it expands the "investigation readiness" demand surface (H4) and creates a new pattern — regulators using AI to identify compliance failures retrospectively, which then triggers investigations LSPs would support.

**Materiality:** 3-4. **Tier 2** — narrow today (hospitals/healthcare), but the pattern is the point. Worth ledger entry.
