## Tier Evaluation

### Microsoft Unveils First Frontier LLM (MAI-Thinking-1), Joining Legal AI Race

Legal IT Insider reports Microsoft has unveiled **MAI-Thinking-1, its first large language model**, claimed to match Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.6, explicitly built to "build frontier AI free from OpenAI." Critically, Legal Tech Monitor's coverage of the Boehmig hire frames it as **"OpenAI joins fellow tech giants Anthropic and Microsoft in developing AI tools for legal work"** — placing Microsoft alongside the two named frontier-legal builders. Combined with Microsoft Scout (M365 personal assistant) and agent-control tooling launched at Build, this is the day's most consequential structural development.

**Materiality: 4. Tier 1.** Yesterday's headline was a *two-vendor* frontier race into legal (OpenAI + Anthropic). Microsoft going model-independent with a frontier-grade LLM — while already owning the M365/Copilot/Purview distribution surface that most law firms run on — converts H5 toward a **three-vendor frontier dynamic**, with Microsoft uniquely positioned on incumbent distribution. For Head of Product: the disintermediation threat now includes the productivity-suite layer your clients already live in, not just standalone legal verticals.

### Anthropic Scales Claude Mythos to Critical Infrastructure in 15+ Countries

TechCrunch and Anthropic report Project Glasswing now extends Mythos access to **150 organizations across 15+ countries**, targeting power, water, healthcare, and communications. This materially revises yesterday's "Mythos US-only" framing — the model is being deployed internationally for critical-infrastructure security, even if not to non-US lenders specifically.

**Materiality: 3. Tier 2.** New, more specific data revising yesterday's geographic-fragmentation entry: Anthropic's geographic restrictions are sector-specific (banking) rather than blanket. Refines the Upstream AI Model Provider Risk theme — supply-chain access is being managed by vertical and use case, not a simple US/non-US line.

### Legal AI Vertical Roll-Up Accelerates: Legora Buys Cadastral; Wordsmith Raises $70M

Two same-day signals of legal AI vendors scaling via capital and acquisition: **Legora's fourth acquisition (Cadastral, a commercial real estate agentic platform used by JLL, AvalonBay, Equity Residential)** moves a horizontal legal AI vendor into a named vertical; **Wordsmith's $70M Series B** (Highland Europe, Index Ventures) funds in-house GC workflow expansion.

**Materiality: 3. Tier 2.** Reinforces vendor consolidation and vertical land-grab dynamics adjacent to LSP/ALSP territory. Legora's named enterprise CRE clients (JLL, AvalonBay) are a concrete data point that legal AI vendors are embedding directly with corporate clients in specific verticals — addressing the "Legora customer base overlap" watchlist item.

### Analyst Note for President/GM and Head of Product

**Three developments today; one is a genuine Tier 1 escalation of the frontier-disintermediation thesis.**

1. **Microsoft has its own frontier LLM and is now named as the third tech giant building legal AI.** This is today's headline. H5 moves from yesterday's *two-vendor* frame (OpenAI + Anthropic) toward a *three-vendor* dynamic — and Microsoft is the most structurally threatening because it can bundle AI into M365/Copilot/Purview, the tools your clients already run. **Action for Head of Product:** elevate Microsoft from a "slow-burn Purview" watchlist item to an active disintermediation vector and watch for a legal-specific MS product built on MAI-Thinking-1.

2. **Anthropic's Mythos is scaling internationally** (150 orgs, 15+ countries) — refining yesterday's "US-only" framing into a sector-gated access model. **Action for President/GM:** treat model-access qualification as per-sector/per-use-case, not a single geographic rule.

3. **Vendor roll-up is accelerating** — Legora's 4th acquisition (named CRE enterprise clients) and Wordsmith's $70M round both compress LSP/ALSP-adjacent vertical and in-house pricing. Watch for further vertical-specialist acquisitions.

**No federal, state, or judicial development meets the Tier 1 threshold today.** The narrower Trump AI-oversight EO (voluntary prerelease review), Colorado's AI Act replacement, and the Connecticut employer-AI transparency law are weekly-synthesis/watchlist items.
