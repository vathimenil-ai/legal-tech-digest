This confirms — rather than changes — the deregulatory pivot already captured in last week's view. The compliance window is now concrete, but the strategic read is unchanged.

Healthcare is the initial scope, but the pattern — a federal agency using AI as a retrospective audit tool — is the part worth watching. If CMS, DOJ FCA, or state AGs follow, it expands the investigation-readiness demand surface meaningfully. Single source today; needs corroboration before it becomes actionable.
