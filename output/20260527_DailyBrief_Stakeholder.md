## What Changed

Nothing at the Tier 1 threshold today. Two secondary items are worth flagging as background context but do not warrant action this morning:

- **Colorado's AI Act replacement is now operational law.** SB 26-189 was signed May 14 and takes effect January 1, 2027, replacing the original Colorado AI Act with a transparency-and-disclosure framework and dropping the mandatory bias audits. This confirms — rather than changes — the deregulatory pivot already captured in last week's view. The compliance window is now concrete, but the strategic read is unchanged.

- **HHS launched the AERO Initiative**, using AI to retrospectively re-score five years of Single Audit Act compliance data for federally-funded entities receiving more than $1M annually. Healthcare is the initial scope, but the pattern — a federal agency using AI as a retrospective audit tool — is the part worth watching. If CMS, DOJ FCA, or state AGs follow, it expands the investigation-readiness demand surface meaningfully. Single source today; needs corroboration before it becomes actionable.
