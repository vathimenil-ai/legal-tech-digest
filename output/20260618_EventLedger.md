## Tier Evaluation

### G7 Heads of State Escalate the Anthropic Shutoff Into a Sovereign-AI / Kill-Switch Crisis

The June 12 Anthropic worldwide shutoff (prior ledger Entry 1) has now produced a materially new development: at the G7 summit, **French President Macron and Indian PM Modi publicly raised that the U.S. can cut off access to American AI overnight** — a fear "the Anthropic blackout just made real" (TechCrunch). India is debating its AI dependence; Canadian pension capital is simultaneously funding Indian data-center buildout. This is no longer a vendor-continuity story — it is now a **head-of-state-level sovereignty and trade-risk story** that will shape how non-US governments, regulators, and multinational clients think about American-model dependence.

**Materiality: 5. Tier 1.** This is the morning's most important new signal because it changes the *altitude* of the supply-chain risk: model-portability and "sovereign fallback" are now diplomatic priorities, which means multinational clients (especially EU/India-exposed) will increasingly demand model-agnostic, jurisdiction-resilient architectures in procurement. For an LSP, this is demand-generative across continuity advisory, multi-model orchestration, and cross-border AI governance — and it hardens H5's "vendor-as-single-point-of-failure" reframe with the strongest possible external validation.

### Anthropic Reframes Restricted-Model Regime as "Project Glasswing" Cyber-Defense Coalition — Broadridge Joins

Following the shutoff, Anthropic published research showing Mythos Preview's "step-change" exploit-development capability and is rolling the restricted model out through **Project Glasswing**, an industry cyber-defense coalition. **Broadridge** publicly joined June 17.

**Materiality: 3. Tier 2.** This clarifies the *mechanism* behind the restricted-access regime — it is being institutionalized as a vetted, membership-gated coalition rather than a temporary lockout. For LSPs, it signals that frontier-model access for sensitive use cases may increasingly run through curated coalitions with security gating, a new procurement and partnership vector (H4/H5).

### Ironclad + Legora "AI-to-AI" Integration; Ironclad Founder Now Leads OpenAI's Legal Vertical

Ironclad (CLM) and Legora announced an unusual "AI-to-AI integration," and Artificial Lawyer confirms Ironclad's former founder **Jason Boehmig has joined OpenAI to launch its legal vertical team**.

**Materiality: 3. Tier 2.** Two signals in one: (1) agent-to-agent interoperability between legal-tech platforms as an emerging architecture, and (2) confirmation OpenAI is staffing a dedicated legal vertical with a credible legal-tech founder — a direct H5 disintermediation data point that pairs with the multi-state OpenAI investigation already tracked.
