## Tier Evaluation

### Thomson Reuters Re-architects CoCounsel Legal — Incumbent Doubles Down on the Assistant Layer

TR has opened early access to what it explicitly frames as the **"next generation" of CoCounsel Legal — the most substantial reworking since the 2023 Casetext acquisition**, with a notable shift in how it describes the product. This is the dominant legal-research/AI incumbent re-platforming its flagship assistant. For an LSP, this matters on the orchestration axis: TR is reinforcing the model-agnostic, workflow-embedded assistant as the competitive surface, and the re-architecture (plus changed positioning language) is a signal about where the research/drafting value layer is consolidating.

**Materiality: 4. Tier 1.** A primary product event from the most significant legal-AI incumbent. Directly relevant to H1 (value shifting to orchestration) and H5 (incumbent vs. frontier-vendor positioning). Worth flagging that "early access / beta" qualifies this as a launch signal, not GA.

### Thomson Reuters "Future of Professionals" — The Adoption-Value Gap Now Has Numbers and Costs

TR's new survey finds **most professionals now use AI at work but far fewer derive value**, and — critically — quantifies the cost: lost clients, departing talent, and slower professional development. Artificial Lawyer independently characterizes adoption as "slow and chaotic." This is primary data that hardens the "Enterprise AI Adoption: Behind the Hype" theme and directly supports the LSP managed-delivery thesis: the gap between deploying AI and extracting value is exactly the gap a governed, orchestrated delivery model closes.

**Materiality: 4. Tier 1.** Quantified, dual-sourced survey data converting an abstract theme into a measurable business cost. Feeds the LSP value narrative (we close the adoption-value gap) and Head of Product's positioning.

### BARBRI Acquires Lega — AI-Governance Startup Pivots to Experiential Training

BARBRI (bar-prep/legal education) acquired **Lega**, the Christian Lang–founded startup that launched in 2023 focused on **LLM governance but pivoted to experiential AI-fluency training**. Triple-sourced (LawSites, Artificial Lawyer, Legal IT Insider).

**Materiality: 3. Tier 2.** The notable signal is the *pivot direction*: a standalone AI-governance tooling startup couldn't sustain that positioning and migrated to training/enablement. A modest data point on whether AI governance is a standalone product or a feature/services layer — relevant to LSP governance-advisory positioning.

### Analyst Note for President/GM and Head of Product

**Two Tier 1 product/market signals from Thomson Reuters today, plus one consolidation data point — and notably, both Tier 1 items come from the dominant incumbent.**

1. **TR is re-platforming CoCounsel Legal (Tier 1).** The most substantial rebuild since Casetext, with repositioning language. The incumbent is reinforcing the assistant/orchestration layer as the battleground — validating our orchestration thesis while raising the bar above which LSP delivery must add value. *Watch GA timing and pricing.*

2. **TR's own survey quantifies the adoption-value gap (Tier 1).** Most professionals use AI; few get value; the shortfall now costs clients and talent. This is the single most usable item this morning: a vendor-neutral, citable statistic that maps exactly onto the LSP "we close the adoption-value gap" narrative. **Action for President/GM:** put this stat in the managed-delivery pitch. **Action for Head of Product:** anchor positioning on realized value, not deployment.

3. **BARBRI/Lega (Tier 2).** A governance-tooling startup pivoted to training and got acquired — a small signal that standalone AI-governance products are hard to sustain, and that governance is migrating into services/enablement.

**Net:** No new theme. The sovereign-AI thread is quiet today; the action returned to the product/market layer, where TR is simultaneously shipping a flagship rebuild and publishing data that — usefully for us — admits the industry isn't yet capturing AI value.
