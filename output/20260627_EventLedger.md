## Tier Evaluation

### Perplexity Launches "Computer for Counsel" — A Second Frontier Vendor Enters the Legal Vertical

Perplexity AI has launched **"Computer for Counsel,"** a legal offering emerging from its own legal department's use of its AI agents, **powered by legal-tech integrations** — and explicitly arriving "weeks after Anthropic expanded its AI capabilities for legal professionals." This is the cleanest single piece of H5 (frontier-vendor disintermediation) evidence in some time: a second general-purpose frontier/search-AI vendor moving directly up the stack into legal, agentic, and integration-led. The "powered by legal-tech integrations" framing matters — it is an orchestration-layer play, not just a chatbot, directly contesting the ground the Standing View identifies as the LSP moat.

**Materiality: 4. Tier 1.** A primary product launch from a frontier vendor entering legal, corroborating the Anthropic-led pattern and hardening the "Frontier AI Vendors as Disintermediation Threat" theme on the platform-launch pathway. *Caveat: single primary outlet (LegalTechMonitor); GA scope and integration depth unconfirmed.*

### Epiq / ClearPeople — Knowledge & Digital-Workspace Modernization Partnership

Epiq (the briefing audience's own company) announced a partnership with **ClearPeople** to "accelerate knowledge and digital workspace modernization." This is a direct Epiq move into the knowledge-management / digital-workspace layer — adjacent to the orchestration and information-governance theses the Standing View tracks. For this specific audience it is decision-relevant context, though as a partnership press release the strategic substance is thin.

**Materiality: 3. Tier 2.** Own-company signal in the knowledge/workspace-modernization adjacency; worth flagging for product strategy but not theme-changing.

### Casepoint Wins $98.8M Department of War BPA for Classified Legal Operations

Casepoint secured an **exclusive $98.8M Department of War BPA** to support classified legal operations. A material government legal-ops/eDiscovery award in the classified segment — a competitive datapoint on where large federal legal-technology spend is consolidating and which vendors hold the security-cleared delivery position.

**Materiality: 3. Tier 2.** Sizeable competitor award in a defensible federal niche; relevant to LSP government-segment positioning.

### Analyst Note for President/GM and Head of Product

**One Tier 1 frontier-vendor signal today, plus two competitive/own-company datapoints.**

1. **Perplexity launches "Computer for Counsel" (Tier 1).** A second frontier/search-AI vendor enters legal within weeks of Anthropic — agentic, integration-led, and built from its own legal department's usage. This is the cleanest disintermediation-pattern evidence we've had: H5 now has two named frontier actors moving up the stack in quick succession, and both are entering at the integration/orchestration layer we claim as our moat. **Action for Head of Product:** read the "powered by legal-tech integrations" framing carefully — they are contesting orchestration directly, not just shipping a chatbot. **Action for President/GM:** treat the Anthropic→Perplexity sequence as a trend, not coincidence, in disintermediation planning.

2. **Epiq/ClearPeople (Tier 2).** Our own move into knowledge/digital-workspace modernization — aligned with the information-governance adjacency. Watch for repeatability.

3. **Casepoint $98.8M DoW BPA (Tier 2).** Large classified federal legal-ops award to a Relativity-alternative vendor; a marker of where defensible government spend concentrates.

**Net:** No new theme, but a meaningful hardening of the frontier-vendor disintermediation thread. The action is back at the vendor/product layer, and the headline is that frontier vendors are now entering legal in a recognizable cadence — at the orchestration layer.
