### Tier 1 Detailed Evaluation

**1. Andrej Karpathy Joins Anthropic's Pre-Training Team**

TechCrunch reports that OpenAI co-founder Andrej Karpathy has joined Anthropic's pre-training team. Karpathy was one of OpenAI's original founding team members and a leading figure in AI research (previously also head of AI at Tesla). Pre-training is described as "responsible for the large-scale training runs that give Claude its core knowledge and capabilities" and is "one of the most expensive, compute-intensive phases of building a frontier model."

**Assessment:** This is a significant talent move between the two leading frontier AI companies. For LSP executives, the implication is twofold: (1) it further strengthens Anthropic's technical bench at a critical moment (Claude for Legal just launched), and (2) it signals continued talent migration away from OpenAI toward Anthropic, reinforcing the capital and talent asymmetry that underpins the standing view's H5 disintermediation thesis. However, this is a personnel move, not a product launch, customer win, or regulatory change. It strengthens an existing thesis directionally but does not change what an LSP executive should do this morning. **Tier 2** — notable signal for standing view confidence but not a "before your first meeting" development.

---

**2. Stilta Raises $10.5M Seed Led by Andreessen Horowitz for Agentic AI Patent Litigation**

LawSites and Legal Tech Monitor report that Stilta, a five-month-old Swedish startup, has raised a $10.5M seed round led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), with participation from Y Combinator and employees from Legora and OpenAI. Stilta claims its "AI agents can do the heavy lifting of patent invalidity and infringement analysis." The company was founded in December 2025 by four former McKinsey consultants.

**Assessment:** This adds a fourth model to the AI-native legal services pattern the standing view tracks: (1) venture-backed AI law firm (Manifest OS $60M), (2) seed-stage AI law firm via MSO (Moritz/Parlai $9M), (3) platform acquisition of ALSP (Carta Law), and now (4) **agentic AI patent litigation startup** (Stilta $10.5M, a16z-backed). The a16z backing is significant — a16z's involvement signals serious capital commitment to AI replacing human legal analysis in patent work, a high-value, high-skill practice area. This directly strengthens H3 (AI compresses revenue per unit) and H5 (disintermediation from frontier vendors moving up-stack). However, Stilta is a five-month-old seed-stage startup with no disclosed clients or outcomes. As a standalone, this is an incremental data point in an established pattern. **Tier 2** — adds to the AI-native legal services model count but is pre-revenue.
