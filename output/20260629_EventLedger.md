## Tier Evaluation

### Asian AI Startups Launch Mythos-Like Models as Anthropic Export Ban Drags On

TechCrunch reports new models launching in Asia promising "Mythos-like capabilities without fear of an export ban," with the framing that "U.S. AI labs may never recover this enormous market." This is a **materially new development** on the BIS/Anthropic-shutoff thread already in the Standing View: the prior signal was a *continuity risk* (a single frontier vendor can be cut off). The new signal is *substitution* — foreign labs are now actively filling the gap created by U.S. export control. For an LSP, this sharpens the upstream-model-provider-risk theme in two directions: (1) it validates that frontier-vendor concentration on a single U.S. lab is a real continuity exposure, and (2) it introduces a sovereignty/provenance complication — non-U.S. substitute models may carry their own jurisdictional, security-clearance, and data-handling constraints that LSPs and their clients must govern. This is exactly the "model/region swap without breaking the audit trail" perimeter the Standing View identifies.

**Materiality: 4. Tier 2 (borderline).** Reinforces H5 and the upstream-provider-risk theme with a genuinely new vector (foreign substitution), but it is a market/macro signal, not a legal-vertical product event. *Caveat: single outlet; no legal-specific adoption data.*

### HP Inc. Scales OpenAI "Frontier" Enterprise Partnership

OpenAI announced HP Inc. is scaling its "Frontier" partnership to deploy AI across customer experience, software development, and enterprise operations. Relevant to H5 (frontier-vendor enterprise penetration) and the OpenAI-vertical-deployment watch item, but the framing is generic enterprise/hardware, with no legal-vertical hook. It is a datapoint on OpenAI's enterprise go-to-market velocity, not a legal-services move.

**Materiality: 3. Tier 2.** Watch-item nourishment for whether OpenAI follows Anthropic/Perplexity into legal; not a legal-vertical entry itself.
