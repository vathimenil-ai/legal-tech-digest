## What Changed

Nothing at the Tier 1 level today. One regulatory item is worth flagging for the watchlist but not for action:

- **What happened:** The European Commission published draft guidelines operationalizing which AI systems qualify as "high-risk" under the EU AI Act, the threshold that triggers the Act's heaviest obligations — conformity assessments, risk management systems, data governance, human oversight, and post-market monitoring.
- **What's new:** Until now, high-risk classification has been a source of compliance ambiguity. The draft guidelines move classification from statutory text toward concrete criteria and worked examples, though the document remains in consultation phase.
- **Why it matters:** The specific examples the Commission ultimately adopts will determine whether common legal AI deployments — employment contract review, e-discovery touching personal data, HR-adjacent tooling — fall inside or outside the Act's heaviest compliance perimeter. For now, this is a watchlist item rather than a procurement-shifting event: draft, not final, and clarifying rather than expanding the existing framework. [Sources: 1]

## Watch Next
