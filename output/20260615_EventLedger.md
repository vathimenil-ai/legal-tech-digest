## Tier Evaluation

### BIS Export Control Forces Anthropic to Shut Off Fable 5 / Mythos 5 Worldwide — Supply-Chain Risk Goes From Hypothetical to Realized

The Standing View has tracked "Anthropic supply chain designation" for weeks as an *unresolved Emerging risk* that *could* slow legal-sector adoption. As of June 12, 2026, BIS issued an export-control directive ordering Anthropic to suspend all access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for any foreign national — and because Anthropic cannot segment its user base by nationality in real time, this resulted in a **complete worldwide shutoff of both models for every customer.** Reporting indicates Amazon's CEO may have been the source of the underlying security concerns; India is publicly debating its AI dependence in response.

**Materiality: 5. Tier 1.** This is the single most important development for an LSP this morning. The frontier-vendor supply-chain risk that was abstract is now concrete and severe: a leading lab can have its core models switched off *globally and without warning* by government action. For any LSP or law firm that has built workflows, managed services, or product on Anthropic models (or on Claude-dependent tools like Harvey, Everlaw MCP, Claude for Legal), this is a live business-continuity and vendor-concentration problem. It directly weaponizes H5 in an unexpected direction — not vendor-moves-up-stack disintermediation, but vendor-as-single-point-of-failure. It is demand-generative (clients will want continuity, multi-model, and vendor-qualification advisory) and it validates governance-first, model-agnostic orchestration as a real differentiator.

### OpenAI Faces Multi-State AG Investigation — Enforcement Vector Broadens Beyond Florida

TechCrunch reports OpenAI now faces an investigation from **multiple state attorneys general**, with questions spanning ad policies and handling of health data. This is distinct from (and an escalation of) the June 4 Florida-sues-OpenAI item: it confirms the "more states will follow" prediction, broadens the enforcement to an investigatory/multi-state coalition, and adds new substantive theories (advertising, health data privacy).

**Materiality: 4. Tier 1.** Combined with the Anthropic shutoff on the same day, both leading US frontier labs are now under simultaneous, distinct government pressure — Anthropic via export controls, OpenAI via multi-state AG investigation. For an LSP this compounds the supply-chain/continuity concern across *both* primary frontier vendors and reinforces investigation-readiness and AI-governance advisory demand. The health-data scope is a specific new hook for regulated-sector clients.

### HaystackID Launches COMET — Off-Channel Capture Productizing a Cross-Atlantic Enforcement Trend

HaystackID introduced COMET, a tool that captures employees' business messages on personal devices on a recurring schedule, positioned around US off-channel-communications enforcement now crossing into Europe, timed to LegalTechTalk London.

**Materiality: 3. Tier 2.** A direct competitor productizing investigation-readiness for off-channel comms — exactly the recurring-revenue, governance-adjacent space the Standing View flags (H4). Watchlist-grade competitive move, not a market-shifter.

### LOD/Consilio + Wordsmith Managed Services — An Incumbent LSP Embeds a Frontier Vendor Into Its Channel

Lawyers On Demand (Consilio-owned) is partnering with Wordsmith to deliver AI-enabled managed services to in-house teams.

**Materiality: 3. Tier 2.** A live data point on the "partner vs. disintermediated" question raised by the Anthropic/Claude Partner Network item: here an established LSP chooses to embed an AI vendor inside its managed-services channel rather than be bypassed. Relevant to H1/H5; refines rather than replaces the channel thesis.

The June 4 "more states will follow" prediction is confirmed. Compounds frontier-vendor procurement caution and investigation-readiness demand.
