## Tier Evaluation

### Ironclad Founder Jason Boehmig Joins OpenAI to Lead Legal Vertical

Artificial Lawyer reports that **Jason Boehmig — founder of CLM pioneer Ironclad — has joined OpenAI to "lead product for the legal vertical."** This converts the prior Low-confidence "OpenAI deployment company" watchlist item into a confirmed, senior, named build. OpenAI is hiring a marquee legal-tech operator with deep CLM/contract domain expertise specifically to lead a legal vertical product effort. This is the second frontier vendor (after Anthropic's Claude for Legal) committing senior leadership and a dedicated vertical product organization to legal.

**Materiality: 5. Tier 1.** This is the most consequential new development today. It transforms H5 (frontier AI disintermediation) from a single-vector confirmed threat (Anthropic) to a **two-vendor frontier race into the legal stack**. The hire of Boehmig specifically signals contract/CLM and likely managed-workflow ambitions — directly overlapping LSP and ALSP service territory. For the President/GM: the disintermediation theme is no longer "Anthropic is moving up the stack"; it is "both leading frontier labs now have dedicated, senior-led legal verticals." For Head of Product: Boehmig's CLM pedigree is a strong signal of where OpenAI will aim first.

### AmLaw 200 Firm Hanson Bridgett Goes All-In With Claude

Artificial Lawyer reports Hanson Bridgett, an AmLaw 200 firm, has **adopted the general Claude model plus legal add-ons firm-wide** ("all-in"). This is the first named AmLaw adoption of Claude for Legal in the feed, directly addressing the Elevated watchlist item: "which law firms adopt Claude for Legal; whether any shift work from LSP-provided review or research."

**Materiality: 4. Tier 1.** This converts the Claude for Legal threat from "product launch" to "named enterprise adoption." A firm-wide commitment from an AmLaw 200 firm is a leading indicator that the 90-agent product surface is being operationalized, not just procured. It tests the CLOC "procurement-ahead-of-adoption" gap and signals that adoption friction may be lower than the 3-5 year operational timeline assumed. For the President/GM: this is the first concrete data point that Claude is winning firm-wide AI standardization decisions — the question is now whether work shifts away from LSP-provided review/research at these firms.

### OpenAI Opens Cybersecurity Model to UK Banks While Anthropic Keeps Mythos US-Only

Finextra reports OpenAI offered the UK's biggest banks access to its new cybersecurity AI model, **explicitly contrasted with Anthropic keeping its Mythos model out of non-US lenders' hands.** This is a concrete new data point on the geographic fragmentation of frontier AI supply chains tied to Anthropic's US supply-chain designation.

**Materiality: 3. Tier 2.** Materially reinforces the "Upstream AI Model Provider Risk" Emerging theme with a primary example: Anthropic's US-only posture creates a vendor-qualification fork for non-US legal buyers, while OpenAI positions itself as the available alternative outside the US. Relevant to global LSP operations and to which model families gain footholds in non-US jurisdictions.
